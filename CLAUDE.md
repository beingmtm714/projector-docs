Publish reviewed public copies only. Exclude the engineering agreement, compensation, contract dates, signatures, credentials, transcripts, and backups. Never merge private repository history into this repository. Review source Markdown and generated HTML before committing.

Build documents with `python3 tools/build-docs.py`. Preserve the existing artboards and design tokens during document updates. The founder will choose the future application direction through section 03 of the product spec.

Product Spec v3 governs build scope. The older plans and engineer brief provide historical context. Younify remains provisional module B21.
